# Level Asset Folder: Temple of Hayana

## Specifications
- **Name**: Temple of Hayana
- **Design**: Maze-like structure
- **Flooring**: Solid stone flooring
- **Ceiling Height**: 15 feet
- **Path Width**: 8 feet wide paths
- **Wall Thickness**: 12-inch thick walls
- **Boundary Size**: 2000 feet wide by 2000 feet deep
- **Acoustics**: Reverberation included

## Mechanics
This dungeon/temple level is loaded when players step south through the Front Porch's centered gate.
The maze is rendered dynamically under 2.5D floor/ceiling scanline projection with solid stone tiles and 15-foot ceilings.
Walk and bark sound effects utilize a feedback-delay reverberation overlay to produce authentic cavernous echoes.

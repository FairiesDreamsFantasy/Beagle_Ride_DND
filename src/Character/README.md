# Character Subsystem

## Overview
The `Character/` directory houses the discrete kinematic, visual, acoustic, and anatomical profiles for all rideable Canines and Riders in Beagle Ride DND.

## Hierarchy
* **Rider/**: Anthropometric specifications, kinematic weight compensation, seating offset geometry, and eye-height camera perspective models.
  * **Fairy-Rider/**: Primary rider (Fairy-Rider nickname), athletic geometry, triangle-pattern onesie, 5'4" stature (5.17 ft POV).
  * **Mary/**: Elegant Cinderella-style rider, puffy shoulders, pink dress with white apron, 6'3" stature (6.0 ft POV).
* **Beagle/**: Quadruped canine locomotion, stride frequencies, baying acoustic profiles, and physical dimensions.
  * **Jetta/**: 5'2" at shoulder, 7 ft length, white with red-orange saddle, collar.
  * **Elsa/**: 5'4" at shoulder, 7 ft length, yellow with dark-pink saddle, dense fur profile.
  * **Thelma/**: 6'0" at shoulder, 8 ft length, red with white saddle, tiara, purple diamond collar, mane.
  * **Tina/**: 5'0" at shoulder, 6.5 ft length, light-blue with dark-pink circular saddle, earrings, green oval collar.
